SIGNAL 75 - UK SITE STRATEGY REVIEW
28 June 2026

Learning only. No picks, scoring, proof, settlement or unlock logic changed.

What the better UK racing sites do:
- Racing Post: horse/tipster table; race location/time; tipster confidence rating; odds; naps/press challenge/top tipsters; result feedback
- Timeform: best bets; tip sheet; analyst verdict per race; flags such as Horse In Focus / Warning Horse / Hot Races; smart stats; ratings based on merit and distance beaten
- At The Races: expert tipsters; Hugh Taylor and named specialists; ratings hub combining speed/form/FormPlus; market movers; racecards/results/replays
- Sporting Life: Timeform-powered tips; Nap-led articles; race previews; going pages; racecards/results/replays; my stable / horses to follow

Main findings for Signal 75:
1. Split evidence into clear buckets, not one mystery score
   Signal 75 action: Keep main Signal 75 score, but show and store separate evidence groups: Find, Confirm, Protect, Learn.
2. Tipster support should count source quality and independence
   Signal 75 action: Give more value to named trusted experts and independent sources; reduce duplicated/soft aggregator consensus.
3. Ratings/form should be checked against race conditions
   Signal 75 action: Course, distance, going, surface, class and field size should become one condition-confidence layer, not four confusing separate warnings.
4. Distance beaten is valuable collateral form
   Signal 75 action: Use winning margins and beaten distances to strengthen horses to follow and caution horses.
5. Post-race comments should explain why, not just what happened
   Signal 75 action: Store whether a horse won easily, was unlucky, weakened, stayed on, failed to respond, or was heavily beaten.
6. Market evidence is a confirmation layer, not a last-minute switch
   Signal 75 action: Track market confidence/drift as learning and warning evidence, but do not confuse users by changing public picks near the off.
7. The best sites separate picks, watchlists, and horses to follow
   Signal 75 action: Keep official proof clean, but make watchlist/follow horses clearer as learning evidence, not betting advice.
8. Beginner-facing wording matters
   Signal 75 action: Translate technical labels into plain English: Find = our score found it; Confirm = outside support; Protect = risks; Learn = what happened after.

Recommended end-July strategy:
1. Find - Signal 75 ranks every runner using price/value, race fit, field size, market data, history and form.
2. Confirm - Trusted tipsters, named experts and market support validate or challenge the Signal 75 view.
3. Protect - Remove or downgrade risky runners: bad recent form, false consensus, weak conditions, too many same-race/same-course picks, chaotic fields.
4. Learn - After racing, store winners, beaten distances, rival evidence, horses to follow, caution horses and repeat patterns.
5. Review - At end of July, only promote rules that repeatedly improved results without harming proof clarity.
