HOW SIGNAL 75 WORKS
Every selection is scored across 9 independent factors.
Nothing is hidden. Every result is published.
75
Qualification Threshold
Every runner starts at a base score of 60.
Nine weighted factors move that score up or down, then the final score is normalised back onto the Signal 75 0-100 scale.
Only horses reaching 75+ can become official picks.
Below 75 = Radar only. Never in the patent. Never in the proof.

POST-SCORE MODIFIER
TIPSTER CONSENSUS
After core scoring is complete, Signal 75 searches OLBG, HorseRacingNet, SportingLife, FreeRacingTips and RacingPost. If public tipsters agree with a selection, it may receive a small confidence boost. If heavily tipped but the market is drifting, it receives a warning flag. The core engine always dominates.
Maximum adjustment: ±3 points
