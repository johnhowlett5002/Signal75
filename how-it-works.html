How To Place The Bet
£1 EACH-WAY PATENT
WHAT TO SELECT AT A BOOKMAKER
At most major bookmakers, including sites such as bet365, go to horse racing multiples and choose a Patent. Add the 3 official Signal 75 horses, select each-way, and choose your stake. Signal 75 proof uses £1 each-way, which is 14 bet lines and £14 total stake. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
3 singles + 3 doubles + 1 treble, all each-way
